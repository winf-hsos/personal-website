## Current Projects

I am curious about how AI can help make teaching more accessible and inclusive. Starting in fall 2026, I will explore this topic in the project **FLEX³**, funded by the [Flexcellence Program](https://www.hs-osnabrueck.de/texas/teilprojekte/flexcellence/) of the University of Applied Sciences Osnabrück.

One goal of this project is to create different AI-dubbed of the same lecture. The two videos below show an example from a Science Slam I held. The version on the left is the original German version, while the version on the right is an English dub created with ElevenLabs AI. The videos are available on YouTube.
